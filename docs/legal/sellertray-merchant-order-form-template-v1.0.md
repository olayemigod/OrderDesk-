SELLERTRAY MERCHANT ORDER FORM

The applicable Merchant Order Form is the Merchant-specific commercial instrument for a SellerTray purchase. It is generated or reused automatically from governed commercial facts; it is not a merchant-specific version of the universal Master Agreement. This Schedule defines the required structure, evidence and legal effect of that Order Form. The actual Agreement Package contains the exact populated Order Form applicable to the Merchant.

## 1. Order Identification

The Order Form records the system-generated unique Order Form identity and immutable version/revision identity, the governed commercial order date and reference, the exact Merchant/Business identity, the applicable currency, and the exact Agreement Package identity and deterministic package fingerprint used for review and confirmation.

## 2. Merchant and Authorised Representative

The Order Form identifies the Merchant legal or registered name, trading name where applicable, business registration details where held, business address, principal contact details and the authenticated representative acting for the Merchant. The representative’s authority is evaluated under the governed SellerTray role/capability model at the time of acceptance or confirmation.

## 3. Purchased Subscription and Entitlement Snapshot

The Order Form records the exact purchased Plan or Plan Profile and applicable immutable/versioned entitlement snapshot, billing frequency, subscription fee, initial Subscription Period or other governed term, renewal terms where applicable, exact included features/capabilities/limits and purchased Add-ons, and identifies Planned/Beta/Preview functionality as non-contractual unless expressly made a current Entitlement.

## 4. Payment-Metering Neutrality

Customer payment method, payment transaction value and payment transaction count are not standard SellerTray Subscription meters. Independent payment-provider fees, separately disclosed SellerTray usage charges or other expressly contracted services may apply without converting ordinary customer payment value or count into a Subscription meter.

## 5. Branches and Setup

The Order Form identifies the Branches included in or purchased under the order, any Branch-specific commercial charge, and whether setup, installation, readiness or another onboarding obligation applies. Operational setup evidence may later record installer, device/serial identifiers, test results, handover condition, Merchant representative, acknowledgement and completion timestamp without rewriting the Order Form.

## 6. Equipment

Where applicable, the Order Form identifies ProcessEdge-supplied Equipment by item, model and quantity; applicable unit and total commercial amount; material accessories; applicable warranty/condition terms; and the relationship of the Equipment to the Qualifying Initial Purchase. Device/serial evidence may be captured at assignment or handover as governed operational evidence without rewriting the historical Order Form.

## 7. Add-ons, WhatsApp, AI and Communications

Purchased Add-ons and any governed WhatsApp, AI, messaging or communications allowances are identified by exact entitlement/allowance and billing basis. AI assists; it does not acquire authority to bypass Merchant permissions, legal acceptance requirements, payment controls, inventory controls or another governed business rule.

## 8. Commercial Summary

The Order Form records the applicable initial Subscription amount, onboarding/setup amount, Branch setup amount, Equipment/accessory amount, installation/delivery amount, initial Add-ons, governed discount or promotion, applicable tax presentation and amount, and exact Total Initial Purchase as derived from the authoritative commercial order and its immutable commercial snapshot.

## 9. Payment Terms and Settlement Are Separate Facts

The Order Form may state invoice, installment or other payment terms and any required payment schedule. Acceptance or confirmation of the Order Form does not by itself prove payment or settlement. Payment status, settlement evidence and entitlement/service-delivery readiness remain separate governed facts.

## 10. 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

Where the purchase qualifies, the Order Form identifies the Qualifying Initial Purchase covered by the 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee. The Guarantee Period begins on the Activation Date, not merely on payment, Account creation or Equipment delivery. The Merchant does not need to prove a defect merely to make a qualifying suitability claim. Reasonable included use during genuine evaluation is not deducted. Required ProcessEdge-supplied Equipment must be returned in Good Condition for the full contractual Guarantee, subject to applicable law. ProcessEdge initiates an approved qualifying refund within 10 Business Days after eligibility verification and, where required, receipt and inspection of returned Equipment.

## 11. Return Transportation

For a voluntary suitability return under the contractual Guarantee, the Merchant bears reasonable return transportation to the designated ProcessEdge return point unless ProcessEdge agrees otherwise. Where return is required because ProcessEdge supplied incorrect Equipment, supplied Equipment with a verified defect for which ProcessEdge is responsible, or caused a material fulfilment error, ProcessEdge bears reasonable return transportation, subject to applicable law.

## 12. Special Commercial Terms

Any Merchant-specific commercial variation must be express, within the lawful scope of the Order Form and authorised through the governed commercial authority. An Order Form does not implicitly override unrelated data-protection, security, intellectual-property, liability, Acceptable Use or other provisions merely because it is Merchant-specific.

## 13. Agreement Package Manifest

The Agreement Package manifest identifies the exact required universal Master Agreement version/content hash, incorporated Schedule/legal-component versions where independently governed, exact Merchant Order Form identity/version, governed commercial snapshot and deterministic package fingerprint. The same package evidence is used to verify that the Merchant signs or confirms exactly what was reviewed.

## 14. Acceptance / Confirmation Mode

**Accept & Continue** is used where the Business has not yet accepted the required Master Agreement version. One affirmative action records the required Master acceptance and confirms the exact Order Form in the reviewed Agreement Package.

**Confirm Purchase** is used for a later purchase under an already accepted unchanged required Master Agreement version. It confirms the exact new Order Form only and does not create another Master Agreement acceptance.

## 15. Electronic Signature and Evidence

The default electronic-signature evidence is the authenticated SellerTray identity, authorised Merchant role/capability, accepted legal name or governed legal-name confirmation, exact Agreement Package ID and fingerprint, Order Form identity/version, relevant Master Agreement acceptance identity where applicable, idempotency command, request evidence and server timestamps. A decorative signature rendering may appear on an executed PDF but is not itself the authority.

## 16. Separate Status Domains

Legal acceptance, Order Form confirmation, commercial order status, payment status, onboarding/setup status, Branch readiness, Activation, Subscription/entitlement status, fulfilment, Guarantee exercise and termination are separate status domains. SellerTray must not collapse them into one status or treat one as proof of another unless the Agreement expressly provides otherwise.

## 17. Durable Review and Executed Copies

SellerTray may generate private server-rendered review and executed Agreement Package PDFs. After valid acceptance/confirmation, the Merchant should have access to a durable electronic copy. The executed copy must remain traceable to the exact immutable Master Agreement acceptance where applicable, Order Form confirmation, Agreement Package manifest/fingerprint and PDF integrity evidence.

## 18. No Silent Editing; Later Changes

An accepted or confirmed Order Form is preserved as historical commercial evidence. Later changes require an authorised amendment, replacement Order Form, Add-on order, renewal instrument or other governed commercial action. SellerTray must not edit the historical Order Form in place or retroactively change the Master Agreement acceptance associated with it.

## 19. Zero-Touch Generation and Admin Exceptions

The normal SellerTray purchase path automatically creates or reuses the exact Order Form and Agreement Package evidence from governed commercial facts. ProcessEdge Admin publishes universal legal versions and reusable governed Order Form templates and handles genuine exceptions; routine merchant purchases do not require an Admin user to manually issue, type or approve a Merchant Order Form one merchant at a time.

## 20. Precedence

This Order Form governs Merchant-specific commercial facts within its proper scope. It does not create a merchant-specific universal Master Agreement. The order of precedence in the Master Agreement continues to apply.
