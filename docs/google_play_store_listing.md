### Full description

SellerTray helps small businesses turn WhatsApp buying conversations into organised orders without forcing customers to install another app.

Customers keep ordering through WhatsApp. SellerTray gives the merchant a simple mobile workspace to review each order, correct what the AI understood, apply merchant-controlled prices and move the order through fulfilment.

With SellerTray you can:

• Receive WhatsApp orders in one organised order inbox.  
• Review AI-assisted item and quantity extraction before accepting an order.  
• Keep prices under merchant control through your SellerTray catalogue.  
• Correct products, quantities and prices when a customer message is unclear.  
• Move orders through review, accepted, processing, ready and completed stages.  
• Reject or cancel orders with a reason and keep a status history.  
• Manage Owner, Manager and Staff access for your business.  
• See today’s order workload and simple business performance insights.  
• Control which order updates are queued for customers on WhatsApp.  
• Export your business data and request account deletion from inside the app.

SellerTray is designed for businesses that already sell through conversations and want a cleaner way to turn those chats into actionable work.

AI is used as an assistant, not as the merchant’s source of truth. SellerTray does not ask AI to invent your prices, stock availability, discounts, delivery charges or payment confirmation. When an order is uncertain, the merchant remains in control of the review and decision.

SellerTray starts with WhatsApp so the customer can keep using the channel they already know while the merchant gets a structured mobile workflow behind the conversation.

SellerTray is provided by ProcessEdge Solutions Limited.

- no currency symbol as the primary mark;
- no “AI” letters;
- no text inside the launcher icon;
- recognisable at small Android launcher size;
- strong silhouette that works on light and dark launchers.
