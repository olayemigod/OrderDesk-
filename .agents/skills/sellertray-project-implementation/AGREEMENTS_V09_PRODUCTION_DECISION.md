# SellerTray v0.9 Production Agreement Decision

## Status and precedence

This file is a **later normative extension** of `.agents/skills/sellertray-project-implementation/SKILL.md` and `AGREEMENTS.md`.

It records the later explicit ProcessEdge decision already preserved in `docs/legal/sellertray-v0.9-production-decision.md` and `docs/legal/sellertray-v0.9-production-artifact-spec.md`.

Where the older **Production legal-content gate for the v0.9 lineage** section in `AGREEMENTS.md` says that v0.9 itself cannot be merchant-published or that first production use necessarily requires a new version code, **this later decision controls**.

Do not reinterpret this decision as external legal advice or external counsel approval. It is ProcessEdge's product/legal-governance decision for SellerTray.

## Approved initial production version

1. **SellerTray Master Services and Subscription Agreement v0.9 is approved as the initial production Master Agreement version.**
2. A new version code is **not required merely to move the approved v0.9 bargain into first production publication**.
3. The retained counsel-review DOCX/PDF remains immutable drafting/audit evidence and must not itself become merchant acceptance truth.
4. Before the first production publication, review-only presentation material may be removed from the merchant-facing v0.9 artifact without minting a new version code, provided the substantive approved bargain is not silently changed.
5. Permitted presentation cleanup includes removal of `External Legal Review Draft`, `NOT FOR MERCHANT ACCEPTANCE`, counsel-review cover/header/footer language, the counsel issue register and internal production-gate material.
6. ProcessEdge's decision not to wait for external counsel removes external counsel as a mandatory deployment dependency. It does not weaken statutory compliance, exact-content approval, immutable publication, AAL2/MFA, capability, audit or production-QA requirements.
7. After v0.9 has been validly published, a material legal/commercial wording change requires a new immutable Master Agreement version. Published v0.9 content and acceptance evidence must never be rewritten in place.
